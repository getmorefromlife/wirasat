## 📖 Overview

The division of inheritance (*Al-Farā’iḍ*) is one of the most intricate and critical fields in Islamic law. The Holy Prophet Muhammad (s.a.w.a.) declared:
> *“Learn the laws of inheritance and teach them to people, for it is half of knowledge, and it is easily forgotten, and it is the first thing that will be taken away from my Ummah.”* — (*Sunan Ibn Majah*)

In Ja‘farī jurisprudence, estate succession is governed by strict mathematical principles, qualitative kinship classes, and ethical safeguards. **Wirasat** bridges traditional seminarian scholarship (*Hawzah*) with modern web engineering to provide:

1. **Zero-Error Distribution Engine:** Instant calculation of complex family estates with 100% adherence to Grand Marāji‘ fatwas.
2. **Gross-to-Net Purification Waterfall:** Complete settlement of funeral expenses, debts, unpaid nuptial gifts (*Mahr*), religious dues (*Khums/Zakat*), and statutory 1/3rd testamentary wills (*Wasiyyah*) before blood heirs inherit.
3. **Dual Legal Causes (*Al-Sabab* vs. *Al-Nasab*):** Dynamic coexistence of surviving spouses (*Sabab*) with bloodline classes (*Nasab*).
4. **The 3 Non-Negotiable Tiers & Absolute Exclusion (*Ḥujub al-Ḥirmān*):** Automatic blocking of distant relatives by nearer generations.
5. **Representation (*Tanzīl*):** Accurate branch representation for grandchildren (Pote, Potiyan, Nawase, Nawasiyan) stepping into the legal shoes of deceased children.
6. **Repudiation of *‘Awl* and *Ta‘ṣīb*:** Protection of Quranic shares against non-Shia arithmetic dilution or agnatic diversion to distant uncles/cousins.
7. **Marja‘ Comparison Modes:** Highlighting authoritative rulings between Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali al-Sistani, Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Abu al-Qasim al-Khoei, and Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali Khamenei (e.g., widow's land valuation rights).
8. **Bilingual Urdu & English Experience:** Native Nastaleeq typography (`font-nastaliq`), right-to-left layout perfection, and comprehensive Urdu juridical terminology.

### 1. 🧮 Interactive 4-Step Inheritance Calculator & Slices Story Engine
- **Step 1: Estate & Purification:** Enter gross estate assets (cash, property, investments) and deduct customary burial (*Kafn wa Tajhīz*), debts, unpaid *Mahr*, religious liabilities (*Khums*), and testamentary wills (enforcing the strict 1/3rd ceiling).
- **Step 2: Deceased Profile:** Set gender and legal attributes.
- **Step 3: Spousal Setup:** Support for surviving husband or multiple wives (up to 4 permanent wives sharing the 1/8 or 1/4 share equally).
- **Step 4: Relatives & Descendants:** Intuitive counters for parents, living sons, living daughters, and multi-tier grandchildren under *Tanzīl* with automated branch inheritance calculations.
- **Plain-Language Slices Story Engine (حسابی تقسیم کی عام فہم وضاحت):** Real-world intuitive translation of mathematical fractions (e.g., explaining why a daughter gets 7/32 and a son gets 21/32 through an intuitive "32 equal slices" story, detailing the surviving spouse's Quranic 1/8 reserve and the remaining 7/8 split under the divine 2:1 ratio).
- **Instant Result Sheet & Breakdown:** Detailed breakdown tables, visual share dials, interactive distribution charts, and printable Sharia distribution certificates.

### 2. 📚 Scholarly Library & Books Hub (کتب خانہ و مصادر)
A verified digital repository under **Knowledge & Academy** featuring downloadable master texts and official Marāji‘ rulings:
- **Direct Master PDF Access & In-Browser Reader:**
  1. *Islāmī Qānūn-e-Virāsat* (`اسلامی قانونِ وراثت`) — Allamah Sayyid Ali Naqi Naqvi (*Naqqan Sahib*) [12.4 MB]
  2. *Tawḍīḥ al-Masā'il Jāmi‘* (`توضیح المسائل جامع — بابِ وراثت`) — Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali al-Husayni al-Sistani [3.7 MB]
  3. *Tawḍīḥ al-Masā'il* (`توضیح المسائل اسلامی احکام — بابِ میراث`) — Grand Ayatollah Sayyid Ali al-Khamenei [6.2 MB]
- **Verified Official Marāji‘ Online Portals:**
  - Grand Ayatollah Sistani's English Legal Code (*Inheritance & Wills*)
  - Grand Ayatollah Sistani's Urdu Legal Code (*بابِ وراثت و ترکہ*)

  - Canonical Hadith Source: *Kitāb al-Mawārīth* from *Al-Kāfī* (Shaykh al-Kulaynī)
  - Classical Masterwork: *Sharā'i‘ al-Islām* (Al-Muḥaqqiq al-Ḥillī)

### 3. 📱 Modern Mobile-First Calculator
- Designed from the ground up for thumb-first usability on smartphones.
- High-contrast accessible color palette, oversized tactile inputs (56px+ tap targets), and slide-up result sheets.
- Instant toggle between simple overview, detailed math, and scholarly notes.

### 4. 🎨 Kinship Whiteboard Studio (استودیو نسب)
- A generative family tree canvas with customizable node relationships and automatic spatial layout.
- **Ready-Made Case Templates (تیار شدہ فقہی خاکے):** Instant loading of empirical real-world scenarios across India, Pakistan, and global Shia communities:
  1. *Standard Nuclear Family (~52%):* Wife, 2 sons, 2 daughters (parents deceased).
  2. *Subcontinent Joint Family (~22%):* Living parents, wife, children, highlighting the *Habwah* rule for the eldest son.
  3. *Daughters Only & Total Exclusion (~14%):* Demonstration of Class I daughters completely barring Class II/III collaterals (brothers/uncles) via Return (*Radd*).
  4. *Orphaned Grandchildren & Tanzīl (~7%):* Grandchildren stepping into their predeceased parent's branch *per stirpes*.
  5. *Childless Couple (~5%):* *Bēwa Bilā Aulād* rules governing movable assets vs. raw land.
  6. *Sole Surviving Daughter (~4%):* 100% estate sole heir barring all collaterals.
  7. *Siblings Only (~3%):* Class II succession without Class I survivors.
  8. *Blank Canvas:* Clean slate builder.
- Real-time simulation of living heirs, deceased parents, and representation branches.
- **Full HD Canvas Export:** High-resolution export of the complete family diagram for legal documentation and family archives.
- **Simultaneous Catastrophe Simulation (*Al-Gharqā wa al-Hadmā*):** Simulating plane crashes or building collapses where the sequence of death is unknown, demonstrating bilateral cross-succession without circular looping.

### 5. 🏛️ 8-Pillar Masterclass Academy (دَائِرَةُ التَّعْلِيمِ وَحِكْمَةُ الْفَرَائِض)
A comprehensive curriculum covering the 8 doctrinal pillars of Ja‘farī inheritance:
- **Pillar I:** Estate Purification Waterfall (Gross vs. Net Estate)
- **Pillar II:** Dual Grounds of Title (*Al-Sabab* vs. *Al-Nasab*)
- **Pillar III:** The 3 Statutory Classes & Total Exclusion (*Ḥujub al-Ḥirmān*)
- **Pillar IV:** Socioeconomic Equilibrium, Gender Shares & Real Estate Rules
- **Pillar V:** Quranic Fractions, Deficit Protection (*Butlan al-‘Awl*) & Reversion (*Al-Radd*)
- **Pillar VI:** Representation (*Tanzīl*) & Eldest Son Relics (*Al-Ḥabwah*)
- **Pillar VII:** Statutory Disqualifications (Homicide, Apostasy/Disparity of Faith, *Li‘ān*, Illegitimacy)
- **Pillar VIII:** Complex Succession Events (Unborn Fetus Escrow, Simultaneous Disasters, *Munāsakhāt*)
- **Practical Real-World Case Studies (عملی فقہی مثال و تجزیہ):** Every pillar includes an audited numerical case study with step-by-step juridical resolution in both English and Urdu Nastaleeq.

## 📥 Getting Started

### Prerequisites
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) (version 18.18 or higher recommended)
- `npm` or `pnpm`

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/getmorefromlife/wirasat.git
   cd wirasat
   ```

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Run the local development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

4. **Open in your browser:**
   Navigate to [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) (or the port indicated in your console, e.g. `http://localhost:3001`).

### Production Build

To verify and compile an optimized production bundle:
```bash
npm run build
npm run start
```
